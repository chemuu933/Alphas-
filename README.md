# Alpha(s) Love and Hope Foundation

A charity website for the Alpha(s) Love and Hope Foundation — a non-governmental organization dedicated to uplifting communities through love, hope, and sustainable development.

## 🌍 Mission
To provide essential support to the needy,less fortunate,disabled and old people in the society.

## 🎯 Goals

- Become a registered NGO
- Be the number one charity organization
- Visit children's homes
- Support cerebral palsy children
- Build resilient communities
- Promote environmental sustainability
