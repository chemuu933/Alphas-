Our Story

Alpha Love and Hope Foundation is a charity organization based in Nakuru, founded by a group of friends united by love, unity, and humanity. The idea began as a birthday celebration for one of the members, scheduled to take place at Phyllis Wambui Memorial Children’s Home in Kabarak, Nakuru, on 24th June 2023. After the event turned out to be a great success, the group developed a strong interest in continuing charitable activities. This led to the formation of a committee comprising the Chief Executive Officer, Chairman, Vice Chairman, Secretary, Treasurer, Communication Manager, Chief Whip, and members. The foundation was officially named Alpha Love and Hope Foundation, and the committee organized a launch event, which was successfully held on 5th August 2023.
